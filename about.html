About

Dynamic Horse Solutions is the shared home of the teaching Jo Ann Lewis and Vanessa Helvey do together, in person in Tucson and on video for everyone who can't make it to the ranch.

The Teachers

Jo Ann Lewis, BSc

Nineteen years as an equine professional, with an emphasis on postural correctness for horse and rider.

Vanessa Helvey, BSc

Equine Osteopath. Masterson Method® Certified Practitioner & Instructor.

Meet Chase, our teacher

Chase was an off-the-track Thoroughbred known to both instructors during his life. Today, his skeleton offers a rare chance to explore equine anatomy in three dimensions, and to understand how structure shapes the living, moving horse.

A horse resting on an open human hand, because, in Jo Ann's words, "the solution comes with human support."
